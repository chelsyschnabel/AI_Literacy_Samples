# AI Literacy: 4th and 5th Grades

## Overview
This demonstration will illustrate how we can encourage students in 4th and 5th grade to begin utilizing AI with their educational needs. This is to help students create a healthy relationship with learning accompanied with AI, particularly utilizing AI to enhance their learning when they do not have the optimal resources available.

**Subject:** Math - Fraction Word Problems  

**Suggested Tool:** ChatGPT, with teacher/parent supervision, or district/school-provided AI tool

**Learning Objective:** Using AI to check understanding and get hints, not answers

## Student Work Sample: "Pizza Party Fractions"

**Original Problem Created by Student:**
"My class is having a pizza party. We ordered 3 pizzas and cut each into 8 slices. If 12 students each eat 2 slices, how many slices will be left over?"

**Student's Initial Work:**
```
3 pizzas × 8 slices = 24 slices total
12 students × 2 slices each = 24 slices eaten
24 - 24 = 0 slices left
```

**AI Interaction for Learning:**
*Student's prompt to AI:* "I solved a pizza problem and got 0 slices left. Can you help me check if my thinking is right? Don't give me the answer, just ask me questions to help me think."

*AI Response:* "Great job showing your work! Let me ask some questions to help you check: How did you figure out the total slices? How did you calculate how many slices the students ate? Does your final answer make sense for a pizza party?"

**Student's Reflection:**
"The AI helped me realize my work was actually correct! At first I thought 0 slices seemed wrong for a party, but when the AI asked me questions, I checked my math again and it was right. The AI didn't give me the answer but helped me feel confident about my thinking."

**Refinement Process:**
Student created two more similar problems and used AI to practice the questioning technique, showing growth in self-checking strategies.
