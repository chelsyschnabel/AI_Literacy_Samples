# AI Literacy: 4th and 5th Grades Rubric
**Total Points:** 12 points

### Mathematical Problem Solving (4 points)
**Exemplary (4 points)**
- Correctly identifies all mathematical operations needed
- Shows clear, organized work with proper units
- Arrives at correct answer through logical steps
- Can explain reasoning verbally or in writing

**Proficient (3 points)**
- Identifies most mathematical operations correctly
- Shows organized work with minor gaps
- Arrives at correct answer or makes only minor errors
- Can explain most of their reasoning

**Developing (2 points)**
- Identifies some appropriate mathematical operations
- Shows some work but may be disorganized
- Makes significant computational errors
- Can explain parts of their reasoning with prompting

**Beginning (1 point)**
- Struggles to identify appropriate operations
- Shows little organized work
- Makes major errors in computation
- Cannot explain reasoning clearly

### AI Interaction Skills (4 points)
**Exemplary (4 points)**
- Asks AI for guidance/hints rather than answers
- Uses AI feedback to check their own thinking
- Demonstrates understanding that AI is a learning helper
- Shows improvement in self-checking after AI interaction

**Proficient (3 points)**
- Generally asks appropriate questions to AI
- Uses most AI feedback effectively
- Shows some understanding of AI as learning tool
- Shows some improvement in confidence/checking

**Developing (2 points)**
- Sometimes asks for hints but may want direct answers
- Uses some AI feedback but inconsistently
- Limited understanding of AI's role in learning
- Little evidence of improved self-checking

**Beginning (1 point)**
- Primarily seeks direct answers from AI
- Difficulty using AI feedback constructively
- Unclear about AI's role in learning process
- No improvement in self-assessment skills

### Communication and Reflection (4 points)
**Exemplary (4 points)**
- Clearly explains problem-solving process
- Thoughtfully reflects on how AI helped their learning
- Uses appropriate mathematical vocabulary
- Shows confidence in defending their work

**Proficient (3 points)**
- Explains most of problem-solving process clearly
- Reflects on AI's role with some insight
- Uses some mathematical vocabulary appropriately
- Shows growing confidence in their work

**Developing (2 points)**
- Explains parts of process but may be unclear
- Basic reflection on AI interaction
- Limited use of mathematical vocabulary
- Shows some uncertainty about their work

**Beginning (1 point)**
- Difficulty explaining problem-solving process
- Minimal reflection on learning experience
- Incorrect or no use of mathematical vocabulary
- Lacks confidence in defending their work

# Assessment Notes for Teachers:
- Focus on process over final answer
- Look for evidence of thinking rather than perfect execution
- Value attempts at self-correction and reflection
- Consider oral assessment for students with writing challenges
