### Core Principles for Peer Evaluation:
- **Be kind and constructive** - Focus on helping your peer learn and improve
- **Be specific** - Use examples from their work to support your feedback
- **Be honest but respectful** - Give truthful feedback in a supportive way
- **Focus on the work, not the person** - Evaluate the project, not your peer
- **Ask questions** - Help your peer think deeper about their work
