# AI Literacy: Undergraduate Computer Science Rubric 
**Total Points:** 200 points

### Research Originality and Contribution (40 points)
**Exemplary (36-40 points)**
- Makes novel contribution to field despite AI assistance
- Uses AI to accelerate development while maintaining research originality
- Demonstrates clear understanding of how work advances existing knowledge
- Shows sophisticated integration of multiple research threads

**Proficient (28-35 points)**
- Makes solid contribution with some AI enhancement
- Generally original work with appropriate AI support
- Shows good understanding of research context
- Adequate integration of existing research

**Developing (20-27 points)**
- Limited contribution despite AI availability
- Difficulty maintaining originality while using AI tools
- Uncertain about research significance
- Poor integration of background research

**Beginning (0-19 points)**
- No clear research contribution
- Work appears derivative or overly AI-dependent
- No understanding of research context
- No integration of existing literature

### Technical Implementation and AI Integration (50 points)
**Exemplary (45-50 points)**
- Sophisticated technical implementation with strategic AI use
- Uses AI tools (Copilot, ChatGPT) to enhance productivity while maintaining code understanding
- Demonstrates mastery of complex algorithms and frameworks
- Clear documentation of AI assistance in development process

**Proficient (35-44 points)**
- Solid technical implementation with appropriate AI support
- Uses AI tools effectively for standard development tasks
- Shows good understanding of implemented algorithms
- Adequate documentation of AI use

**Developing (25-34 points)**
- Basic technical implementation with inconsistent AI integration
- Limited understanding of code despite AI assistance
- Difficulty explaining implemented algorithms
- Poor documentation of AI involvement

**Beginning (0-24 points)**
- Inadequate technical implementation
- Over-reliance on AI without understanding
- Cannot explain implemented solutions
- No documentation of AI assistance

### Experimental Design and Analysis (40 points)
**Exemplary (36-40 points)**
- Rigorous experimental design with AI-enhanced analysis
- Appropriate statistical methods with AI support for interpretation
- Comprehensive evaluation across multiple metrics and datasets
- Sophisticated understanding of experimental limitations

**Proficient (28-35 points)**
- Adequate experimental design with some AI assistance
- Generally appropriate statistical methods
- Reasonable evaluation approach
- Shows awareness of key limitations

**Developing (20-27 points)**
- Flawed experimental design despite AI availability
- Basic statistical methods with interpretation errors
- Limited evaluation approach
- Little understanding of experimental limitations

**Beginning (0-19 points)**
- Poor experimental design
- Inappropriate or no statistical analysis
- Inadequate evaluation methodology
- No understanding of limitations

### Academic Integrity and Research Ethics (35 points)
**Exemplary (32-35 points)**
- Exemplary transparency about AI use throughout research process
- Maintains highest standards of academic integrity
- Demonstrates sophisticated understanding of AI ethics in research
- Provides complete documentation for reproducibility

**Proficient (25-31 points)**
- Good transparency about AI assistance
- Maintains academic integrity with minor gaps
- Good understanding of research AI ethics
- Adequate documentation for reproducibility

**Developing (18-24 points)**
- Some transparency issues about AI use
- Unclear boundaries around academic integrity
- Basic understanding of AI research ethics
- Limited documentation

**Beginning (0-17 points)**
- Serious transparency issues
- Academic integrity concerns
- No understanding of AI ethics in research
- Inadequate documentation

### Professional Communication and Impact (35 points)
**Exemplary (32-35 points)**
- Excellent research communication suitable for academic publication
- Demonstrates real-world impact and applications
- Sophisticated reflection on AI's role in research process
- Makes code and methods available to research community

**Proficient (25-31 points)**
- Good research communication with clear findings
- Shows potential for practical application
- Good reflection on research process and AI use
- Shares work appropriately

**Developing (18-24 points)**
- Adequate communication with some unclear elements
- Limited consideration of practical applications
- Basic reflection on research experience
- Minimal sharing of research outputs

**Beginning (0-17 points)**
- Poor research communication
- No consideration of practical impact
- No meaningful reflection on research process
- No sharing of research contributions
