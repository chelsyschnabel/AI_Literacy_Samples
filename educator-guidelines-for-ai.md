# Assessment Guidelines for Educators

## AI Literacy Progression Indicators

### Elementary Level Focus:
- AI as learning helper rather than answer provider
- Basic understanding that AI can make mistakes
- Beginning ability to explain their thinking process
- Comfort asking AI for hints and guidance

### Middle School Level Focus:
- Strategic use of different AI tools for different purposes
- Understanding of AI limitations in research contexts
- Ability to evaluate AI suggestions critically
- Clear documentation of AI assistance

### High School Level Focus:
- Sophisticated integration of AI into complex analytical work
- Understanding of AI bias and reliability issues
- Ability to maintain academic integrity while maximizing AI benefits
- Reflection on AI's impact on learning and thinking

### Undergraduate Level Focus:
- Professional-level AI integration for research acceleration
- Deep understanding of AI ethics in academic/professional contexts
- Ability to use AI while maintaining research originality
- Contribution to knowledge about effective AI use in the field

## Common Assessment Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Over-penalizing AI use** - Focus on learning enhancement rather than AI avoidance
2. **Under-documenting AI interactions** - Require transparency without making it burdensome
3. **Ignoring process for product** - Value learning journey over perfect final results
4. **One-size-fits-all standards** - Adjust expectations for developmental appropriateness
5. **Static assessment approaches** - Update rubrics as AI capabilities evolve

## Recommendations for Implementation

- **Start with clear expectations** about AI use and documentation
- **Model appropriate AI interactions** in class before independent work
- **Create safe spaces** for discussing AI mistakes and learning from them
- **Regularly update assessment criteria** as AI tools and student capabilities evolve
- **Focus on growth over perfection** in AI literacy development
- **If your district has an AI selected for use** consider ways to integrate those provided tools alongside of or instead of suggested tools
