# AI Literacy: High School Statistics Research Project Rubric  
**Total Points:** 150 points

### Literature Review and Source Evaluation (25 points)
**Exemplary (23-25 points)**
- Comprehensive literature review using AI-enhanced search strategies
- Critically evaluates sources with AI assistance while maintaining independent judgment
- Synthesizes multiple perspectives effectively
- Demonstrates sophisticated understanding of academic credibility

**Proficient (18-22 points)**
- Adequate literature review with some AI enhancement
- Generally evaluates sources appropriately
- Shows some synthesis of different viewpoints
- Good understanding of source credibility

**Developing (13-17 points)**
- Limited literature review despite AI availability
- Basic source evaluation with over-reliance on AI judgment
- Little synthesis of multiple perspectives
- Uncertain about source quality assessment

**Beginning (0-12 points)**
- Inadequate literature review
- Poor source evaluation even with AI assistance
- No synthesis of different viewpoints
- No understanding of academic source standards

### Statistical Analysis and AI Integration (40 points)
**Exemplary (36-40 points)**
- Sophisticated statistical analysis with appropriate AI support
- Demonstrates deep understanding of statistical concepts through AI interactions
- Uses AI to enhance analysis while maintaining mathematical reasoning
- Correctly interprets complex statistical outputs with AI guidance

**Proficient (28-35 points)**
- Adequate statistical analysis with AI assistance
- Shows good understanding of key statistical concepts
- Uses AI appropriately to support analysis
- Generally interprets statistical results correctly

**Developing (20-27 points)**
- Basic statistical analysis with inconsistent AI integration
- Limited understanding of statistical concepts despite AI help
- Sometimes uses AI inappropriately or ineffectively
- Misinterprets some statistical results

**Beginning (0-19 points)**
- Inadequate statistical analysis
- No evidence of understanding statistical concepts
- Inappropriate use of AI for statistical work
- Cannot interpret statistical outputs correctly

### Research Design and Methodology (25 points)
**Exemplary (23-25 points)**
- Well-designed study with clear methodology
- Uses AI thoughtfully to refine research approach
- Addresses potential confounding variables appropriately
- Shows sophisticated understanding of research limitations

**Proficient (18-22 points)**
- Adequate research design with some AI input
- Generally sound methodology
- Recognizes some potential confounding factors
- Shows awareness of basic research limitations

**Developing (13-17 points)**
- Flawed research design despite AI availability
- Basic methodology with significant gaps
- Little consideration of confounding variables
- Limited understanding of research limitations

**Beginning (0-12 points)**
- Poor research design
- Inadequate methodology
- No consideration of confounding factors
- No understanding of research limitations

### Data Interpretation and Critical Thinking (30 points)
**Exemplary (27-30 points)**
- Sophisticated interpretation of findings
- Uses AI to explore multiple explanations while maintaining critical judgment
- Clearly distinguishes correlation from causation
- Demonstrates advanced critical thinking about implications

**Proficient (21-26 points)**
- Adequate interpretation of results
- Shows some critical thinking with AI support
- Generally understands correlation vs. causation
- Shows reasonable judgment about implications

**Developing (15-20 points)**
- Basic interpretation with some errors
- Limited critical thinking despite AI availability
- Confused about correlation and causation
- Simplistic thinking about implications

**Beginning (0-14 points)**
- Poor interpretation of findings
- No evidence of critical thinking
- Cannot distinguish correlation from causation
- No understanding of research implications

### Academic Writing and AI Ethics (30 points)
**Exemplary (27-30 points)**
- Excellent academic writing enhanced by thoughtful AI use
- Complete transparency about AI assistance in research process
- Maintains academic integrity throughout
- Sophisticated understanding of AI ethics in academic work

**Proficient (21-26 points)**
- Good academic writing with appropriate AI support
- Generally transparent about AI use
- Maintains academic integrity with minor issues
- Good understanding of AI ethics

**Developing (15-20 points)**
- Adequate writing with inconsistent AI integration
- Some transparency about AI use but incomplete
- Some concerns about academic integrity boundaries
- Basic understanding of AI ethics

**Beginning (0-14 points)**
- Poor academic writing
- Little to no transparency about AI assistance
- Serious academic integrity concerns
- No understanding of ethical AI use
