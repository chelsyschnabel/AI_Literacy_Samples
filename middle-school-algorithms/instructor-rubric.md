# AI Literacy: 7th and 8th Grades Rubric  
**Total Points:** 60 points

### Algorithm Logic and Design (20 points)
**Exemplary (18-20 points)**
- Creates logically sound algorithm with clear steps
- Demonstrates understanding of sequence, selection, and iteration
- Algorithm is complete and would function as intended
- Shows evidence of planning and systematic thinking

**Proficient (14-17 points)**
- Creates mostly sound algorithm with minor logical gaps
- Shows good understanding of basic programming concepts
- Algorithm would mostly function with minor corrections
- Shows some evidence of systematic approach

**Developing (10-13 points)**
- Creates algorithm with some logical errors or missing steps
- Basic understanding of programming concepts
- Algorithm has significant gaps but shows some correct thinking
- Limited evidence of systematic planning

**Beginning (0-9 points)**
- Algorithm has major logical flaws or is incomplete
- Little understanding of programming concepts shown
- Algorithm would not function as intended
- No evidence of systematic thinking or planning

### AI-Assisted Improvement Process (15 points)
**Exemplary (14-15 points)**
- Uses AI strategically to identify areas for improvement
- Asks thoughtful questions about user experience and algorithm efficiency
- Incorporates AI feedback while maintaining ownership of design decisions
- Shows clear progression from initial to refined algorithm

**Proficient (11-13 points)**
- Uses AI appropriately to get feedback on algorithm
- Asks reasonable questions about improvements
- Generally incorporates useful AI suggestions
- Shows some improvement from initial design

**Developing (8-10 points)**
- Uses AI but with limited strategic thinking
- Asks basic questions or seeks direct answers
- Inconsistently incorporates AI feedback
- Limited improvement shown from initial to final

**Beginning (0-7 points)**
- Inappropriate use of AI or seeks complete solutions
- No evidence of strategic questioning
- Does not incorporate AI feedback effectively
- No meaningful improvement demonstrated

### Problem-Solving and User Consideration (15 points)
**Exemplary (14-15 points)**
- Demonstrates sophisticated thinking about user experience
- Considers edge cases and error handling
- Shows understanding of game balance and fairness
- Thinks beyond basic functionality to user engagement

**Proficient (11-13 points)**
- Shows good consideration of user needs
- Addresses some edge cases or potential issues
- Understands basic principles of game design
- Goes somewhat beyond minimum requirements

**Developing (8-10 points)**
- Shows basic awareness of user needs
- Limited consideration of edge cases
- Basic understanding of game functionality
- Meets minimum requirements with little extension

**Beginning (0-7 points)**
- No evidence of user consideration
- No awareness of potential problems or edge cases
- Does not understand basic game design principles
- Does not meet minimum requirements

### Communication and Reflection (10 points)
**Exemplary (9-10 points)**
- Clearly explains algorithm design choices and reasoning
- Thoughtfully reflects on how AI helped improve their thinking
- Uses appropriate technical vocabulary
- Demonstrates growth in algorithmic thinking

**Proficient (7-8 points)**
- Explains most design choices adequately
- Shows some reflection on AI's role in improvement process
- Uses some technical vocabulary appropriately
- Shows some evidence of learning

**Developing (5-6 points)**
- Explains some design choices but may be unclear
- Basic reflection on AI interaction
- Limited use of appropriate vocabulary
- Little evidence of learning or growth

**Beginning (0-4 points)**
- Cannot explain design choices clearly
- No meaningful reflection on learning process
- Incorrect or no use of technical vocabulary
- No evidence of understanding or growth
