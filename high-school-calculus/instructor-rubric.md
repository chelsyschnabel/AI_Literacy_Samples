# AI Literacy: Calculus Rubric
**Total Points:** 80 points

### Mathematical Problem Solving (25 points)
**Exemplary (23-25 points)**
- Sets up optimization problem correctly with proper variables and constraints
- Uses calculus techniques (derivatives, critical points) accurately
- Shows all mathematical work clearly and logically
- Arrives at correct solution through valid mathematical reasoning

**Proficient (18-22 points)**
- Generally sets up problem correctly with minor issues
- Uses calculus techniques mostly accurately
- Shows most mathematical work with some gaps
- Arrives at correct or nearly correct solution

**Developing (13-17 points)**
- Sets up problem with some errors in variables or constraints
- Uses calculus techniques with significant errors
- Shows some mathematical work but with major gaps
- Solution has significant mathematical errors

**Beginning (0-12 points)**
- Cannot set up optimization problem correctly
- Major errors in calculus techniques
- Little coherent mathematical work shown
- No valid mathematical solution attempted

### Strategic AI Integration (20 points)
**Exemplary (18-20 points)**
- Uses different AI tools strategically for different purposes (verification vs. conceptual understanding)
- Maintains independent mathematical thinking while leveraging AI capabilities
- Shows sophisticated understanding of when and how to use AI assistance
- Demonstrates growth in mathematical confidence through AI interaction

**Proficient (14-17 points)**
- Uses AI tools appropriately for problem-solving support
- Generally maintains independence in mathematical reasoning
- Shows good judgment about AI assistance timing
- Shows some growth in mathematical understanding

**Developing (10-13 points)**
- Uses AI inconsistently or inappropriately
- Sometimes relies too heavily on AI for basic mathematical thinking
- Limited understanding of strategic AI use
- Little evidence of growth through AI interaction

**Beginning (0-9 points)**
- Inappropriate AI use or over-dependence on AI solutions
- No evidence of independent mathematical thinking
- No understanding of appropriate AI integration
- No learning growth demonstrated

### Conceptual Understanding and Extension (20 points)
**Exemplary (18-20 points)**
- Demonstrates deep understanding of optimization principles
- Uses AI to explore conceptual connections and real-world applications
- Extends problem to consider variations or related scenarios
- Shows sophisticated mathematical intuition development

**Proficient (14-17 points)**
- Shows good understanding of optimization concepts
- Uses AI appropriately to clarify mathematical ideas
- Shows some extension or connection to applications
- Demonstrates developing mathematical intuition

**Developing (10-13 points)**
- Basic understanding of optimization with significant gaps
- Limited use of AI for conceptual development
- Little extension beyond basic problem requirements
- Weak mathematical intuition development

**Beginning (0-9 points)**
- Poor understanding of optimization concepts
- No evidence of AI-assisted conceptual learning
- No extension or application thinking
- No development of mathematical intuition

### Mathematical Communication and Verification (15 points)
**Exemplary (14-15 points)**
- Clearly communicates mathematical reasoning and solution process
- Uses AI verification thoughtfully to confirm accuracy
- Explains why solution makes intuitive sense in problem context
- Demonstrates metacognitive awareness of problem-solving process

**Proficient (11-13 points)**
- Communicates mathematical work adequately
- Uses AI verification appropriately
- Shows some intuitive understanding of solution
- Some evidence of metacognitive thinking

**Developing (8-10 points)**
- Communication of mathematical work is unclear or incomplete
- Basic use of AI verification with limited understanding
- Little intuitive grasp of solution meaning
- Limited metacognitive awareness

**Beginning (0-7 points)**
- Cannot communicate mathematical reasoning clearly
- No meaningful use of AI verification
- No understanding of solution significance
- No metacognitive awareness demonstrated
