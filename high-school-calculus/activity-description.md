# AI Literacy: Calculus

## Overview

In this analysis, students will utilize AI to expand their understanding of their solutions for mathematical computations. In this example, students utilize the AI not to solve the problem for them, but to help them understand the reason as to why their solutions are correct for the problem provided.

**Subject:** Math - Calculus Problem Solving  

**Suggested Tools:** Desmos & ChatGPT for different purposes 

**Learning Objective:** Using AI tools strategically for verification and concept clarification

## Student Work Sample: "Optimization Problem Analysis"

**Problem:** A farmer wants to fence a rectangular field adjacent to a river. No fence is needed along the river. If the farmer has 240 feet of fencing, what dimensions maximize the area?

**Student's Solution Process:**

**Step 1: Independent Problem Setup**
```
Let x = width (perpendicular to river)
Let y = length (parallel to river)
Constraint: 2x + y = 240 (total fencing)
Objective: Maximize A = xy
```

**Step 2: Mathematical Work**
```
From constraint: y = 240 - 2x
Substitute: A(x) = x(240 - 2x) = 240x - 2x²
Find critical points: A'(x) = 240 - 4x = 0
Solving: x = 60
Therefore: y = 240 - 2(60) = 120
Maximum area = 60 × 120 = 7200 sq ft
```

**Step 3: AI-Assisted Verification**
*Wolfram Alpha query:* "maximize x(240-2x) for x>0"
*Result confirmed:* x = 60, maximum value = 7200

**Step 4: Concept Clarification with ChatGPT**
*Student's prompt:* "I solved an optimization problem and got x=60, y=120. My answer checks out mathematically, but I want to understand WHY this makes intuitive sense. Can you help me think about the relationship between width and length in fencing problems?"

*Key insight gained:* The optimal rectangle is always twice as long as it is wide when one side needs no fencing - this creates the most efficient use of available perimeter.

**Step 5: Extension and Deeper Learning**
Student created variations: "What if the river curved?" and used AI to explore how the mathematical model would change, demonstrating transfer of learning.

**Reflection on AI Use:**
"I used AI in two different ways: Desmos as a calculator to check my algebra, and ChatGPT as a tutor to help me understand the 'why' behind my answer. Neither tool did my thinking for me, but both helped me verify and deepen my understanding. This is how I want to use AI in college math courses."
