# AI Literacy Work Samples for Math & Computer Science Education

A comprehensive collection of educational activities, assessments, and resources for developing AI literacy across K-12 through higher education in mathematics and computer science contexts.

## 🎯 Purpose

These work samples demonstrate how students can use AI as a **learning enhancement tool** rather than a replacement for critical thinking. Each activity shows students leveraging AI to refine their work, check their understanding, and deepen their learning while maintaining academic integrity and intellectual ownership.

## 📖 Available Activities

### Elementary Level (4th-5th Grade)
**📁 `elementary-fractions/`**
- **Subject**: Mathematics - Fraction Word Problems
- **AI Integration**: Using ChatGPT as a questioning partner for self-checking
- **Learning Focus**: AI as a learning helper, not answer provider
- **Duration**: Single class period

### Middle School (6th-8th Grade)

**📁 `middle-school-algorithms/`**
- **Subject**: Computer Science - Algorithm Design
- **AI Integration**: Claude for pseudocode review and improvement
- **Learning Focus**: Using AI to enhance algorithm design thinking
- **Duration**: 1-2 weeks

### High School (9th-12th Grade)

**📁 `high-school-calculus/`**
- **Subject**: Mathematics - Calculus Optimization
- **AI Integration**: Wolfram Alpha & ChatGPT for verification and conceptual understanding
- **Learning Focus**: Strategic AI use for mathematical learning
- **Duration**: 1-2 weeks

**📁 `high-school-research/`**

## 🎓 Educational Philosophy

### Core Principles
- **Learning Enhancement**: AI amplifies rather than replaces student thinking
- **Academic Integrity**: Complete transparency in AI use and documentation
- **Critical Thinking**: Students evaluate AI suggestions rather than accepting blindly
- **Progressive Development**: Sophistication increases with educational level
- **Authentic Assessment**: Focus on learning process and growth over perfect products

### AI Literacy Development
Each activity develops key AI literacy competencies:
- **Strategic Tool Selection**: Choosing appropriate AI tools for different tasks
- **Prompt Engineering**: Crafting effective questions and requests
- **Critical Evaluation**: Assessing AI outputs for accuracy and usefulness
- **Ethical Integration**: Understanding boundaries of appropriate AI use
- **Reflective Practice**: Metacognitive awareness of AI's role in learning

## 🚀 Getting Started

### For Educators
1. **Start with Guidelines**: Read `educator-guidelines-for-ai.md` for implementation strategies
2. **Choose Your Level**: Navigate to the appropriate grade-level folder
3. **Review Activity**: Read the `activity-description.md` for complete project details
4. **Prepare Assessment**: Use the instructor rubric to understand evaluation criteria
5. **Introduce to Students**: Share self-evaluation and peer-evaluation forms with students

### For Students
1. **Read Activity Description**: Understand the project goals and requirements
2. **Use AI Thoughtfully**: Focus on learning enhancement, not task completion
3. **Document Your Process**: Be transparent about AI interactions
4. **Self-Assess Regularly**: Use provided self-evaluation rubrics
5. **★ Engage in Peer Review ★**: Provide constructive feedback to classmates

★ Students will not peer review research projects.
